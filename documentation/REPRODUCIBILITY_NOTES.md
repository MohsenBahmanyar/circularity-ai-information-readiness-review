# Reproducibility notes

The two evidence streams retain distinct eligibility, screening, and extraction rules. The primary circularity-information review supplies requirement, source, timing, and readiness evidence. The targeted supplementary AI evidence review supplies demonstrated Target/Output capability and supporting-role evidence. The streams are integrated only at the normalized-attribute level.

The public package contains derived bibliographic metadata, decisions, codebooks, evidence locators, and paraphrased notes. It excludes publisher PDFs, full copyrighted abstracts, and proprietary database exports. Exact search strings, filters, counts, and export filenames are retained in the derived search workbooks; original exports remain in the private archive.
